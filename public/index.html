Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a water leak?

Navigate to the "Report" screen from the bottom menu, attach photo evidence, add description details, and submit.

Who has access to the Sarpanch task view?

Only whitelisted emails mapped to the "Sarpanch" or "Admin" roles can view and mark issues as resolved.

How long does water system repairs take?

Critical system pipeline leakages are addressed immediately. Public handpump welder repairs usually take 1-3 days.
