Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a water leak? expand_more
Use the 'Report' tab in the bottom navigation. You can take a photo, select the location, and add details to help our maintenance team respond quickly.
When is trash collection day? expand_more
Trash is collected every Tuesday and Friday morning. Please place your bins at the curb by 7:00 AM.
How long do repairs usually take? expand_more
Routine maintenance tasks are typically completed within 3-5 business days. Emergency repairs (like major water leaks) are prioritized for immediate dispatch.
